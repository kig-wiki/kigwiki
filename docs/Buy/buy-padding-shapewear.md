# Where to Buy Kigurumi Body Padding and Shapewear

Body padding and shapewear are not specific to Kigurumi. Useful products usually aren't marketed specifically for Kigurumi but instead may be marketed to a wide variety of use cases from typical women's shapewear, to theatrical performance, to more niche specifics like crossdressing or beyond.

Read [Kigurumi body padding theory](../Start/padding-theory.md) before choosing a product.

:::warning

Some linked stores display breast forms, anatomical products, or adult-oriented merchandise.

:::

The stores below are starting points, not endorsements or a complete market survey.

## Padding

### Roanyer

[Roanyer](https://www.roanyer.com/) sells silicone and foam forms, and also operates a [Tmall store](https://roanyer.world.tmall.com/shop/view_shop.htm). International orders from Tmall may require a proxy service.

### Moli's / InThemask

[Moli's / InTheMask](https://inthemask.com/) sells silicone padding along with other transformation products like 'fem masks'.

### General marketplaces

Amazon, AliExpress, and Taobao carry basic foam padding types and breast forms. Quality and dimensions vary, so check customer photos and return terms.

Foam is often the cheapest and most accessible material for most padding types. That doesn't mean it's the worst option, just the most common for most padding that isn't breast forms.

## Shapewear

### Maidenform

[Maidenform](https://www.maidenform.com/shapewear) sells widely available waist, torso, and lower-body shapewear. Do be mindful of any visible seams or closures that may show through the hadatai, such as if there are visible stomach closures for a character that may have a "bare" stomach.

### Other sellers on Amazon, Aliexpress, and Taobao

Akin to the mention above on padding, you can find a lot of shapewear on Amazon, Aliexpress, and Taobao. They are usually cheap and come in a variety of sizes. YMMV on quality and durability, we plan to add a more comprehensive list of shapewear sellers in the future.
